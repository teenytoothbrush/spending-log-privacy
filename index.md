# Japan Spending Log Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Japan Spending Log is a local-first trip cash and spending tracker.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores your transactions, starting-cash setting, categories, optional notes, and optional purchase photos only on your device. Japan Spending Log does not provide accounts, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or a way to share this data with the developer.

Camera permission is requested only when you choose to take a purchase photo. Selected or captured photos are stored privately in the app's local storage.

## USD-to-JPY reference-rate requests

When you explicitly choose to fetch or refresh the USD-to-JPY rate, Japan Spending Log requests an ECB reference rate from Frankfurter at `https://api.frankfurter.dev`. The request does not include your transactions, photos, or other ledger data. As with any internet request, the service and its infrastructure providers may receive standard network information, such as your IP address, to operate the request.

The app does not refresh rates automatically or in the background. A rate is informational only and may differ from the rate offered by a bank, card provider, ATM, or exchange counter.

## Your choices

You may deny camera permission and continue using the app without purchase photos. You may use the app without fetching a reference rate. You can remove all locally stored app data through Android's app settings or by uninstalling the app.
